Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 3, 2026 • App: StyloSynth (com.stylosynth.app)

StyloSynth ("we", "our", "us") is a stylus-synth instrument app. This policy explains what data the app and its advertising provider collect, why, and your choices. The app itself has no accounts, no sign-in, and no servers of its own.

1. Data we collect

The app does not directly collect names, emails, or precise location. Through Google AdMob advertising, the following may be collected automatically on your device:

  • Advertising ID (com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID) and device identifiers for ad personalization, frequency capping, and fraud prevention.
  • Device & network info: device model, OS version, app version, IP-based approximate location, network state.
  • App interactions & diagnostics: ad impressions/clicks, performance and crash data used to measure and improve ads.

2. How data is used

  • To serve, measure, and improve banner and interstitial ads (including consent-based personalized ads where you agree).
  • To prevent fraud, enforce frequency limits, and comply with legal obligations.

3. Sharing — Google AdMob

Ads are provided by Google AdMob (Google LLC). Data above is shared with Google under its policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses data from apps that use its services
  • AdMob & the EU User Consent Policy

We do not sell your personal data. We do not share data with other third parties.

4. Consent (GDPR / EEA, UK, Switzerland & US states)

  • EEA/UK/CH: on first launch the app uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to request consent for personalized ads where required. You can change your choice anytime by reinstalling the app (which resets the stored consent) or via your device's ad settings.
  • US states (e.g. California, Virginia, Colorado): applicable opt-out / "Do Not Sell or Share" messages are shown where required, and Google honors Global Privacy Control signals.
  • You can always limit ad tracking in Android: Settings → Privacy → Ads → Delete or reset Advertising ID.

5. Children

StyloSynth is a general-audience music instrument and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child provided data via ads, contact us and we will help address it with Google.

6. Retention & security

We store no personal data on our own servers (there are none). Ad-related data is retained by Google per its policies. Transmission is encrypted (HTTPS).

7. Your rights

Depending on your region (GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, etc.) you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to processing of your data, and to withdraw consent. For ad data held by Google, exercise these via Google's controls and your device settings; contact us below and we will reasonably assist.

8. Contact

Questions or requests: stylosynth.support@gmail.com. Please include your device model, app version, and country so we can help.

9. Changes

We will post policy updates on this page and update the effective date. Material changes will also be reflected in the Play Store listing.
